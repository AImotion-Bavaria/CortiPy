# CortiPy Code of Conduct

We follow the Contributor Covenant 2.1. Be respectful, inclusive, and assume good intent. We do not tolerate harassment, discrimination, or demeaning behavior in any community space (issues, PRs, chat, meetings, events).

## Standards
- Welcome newcomers; be clear, kind, and patient.
- Offer constructive feedback; critique ideas, not people.
- Respect boundaries; no unsolicited contact or sharing of sensitive data.
- Keep clinical discussions professional and privacy-conscious; never share identifiable participant data.

## Reporting
- Email joh1391@thi.de for behavior concerns.
- Email joh1391@thi.de, Rahul.Mondal@thi.de or Laurens.Kreilinger@thi.de for clinical/academic collaboration concerns.
- Reports are confidential; maintainers will acknowledge receipt within 5 business days and follow up with next steps.

## Enforcement
- Maintainers may warn, mute, or remove participants; repeated or severe violations may result in bans.
- Appeals can be sent to joh1391@thi.de.
